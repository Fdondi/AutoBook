### Chapter 3

| Rotations | Rigid-Body Motions |
|---|---|
| $R \in SO(3) : 3 \times 3$ matrices<br>$R^{\mathrm{T}} R = I, \det R = 1$ | $T \in SE(3) : 4 \times 4$ matrices<br>$T = \begin{bmatrix} R & p \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$,<br>where $R \in SO(3), p \in \mathbb{R}^3$ |
| $R^{-1} = R^{\mathrm{T}}$ | $T^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} R^{\mathrm{T}} & -R^{\mathrm{T}}p \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$ |
| change of coordinate frame:<br>$R_{ab}R_{bc} = R_{ac}, \quad R_{ab}p_b = p_a$ | change of coordinate frame:<br>$T_{ab}T_{bc} = T_{ac}, \quad T_{ab}p_b = p_a$ |
| rotating a frame {b}:<br>$R = \mathrm{Rot}(\hat{\omega}, \theta)$<br>$R_{sb'} = RR_{sb}$:<br>rotate $\theta$ about $\hat{\omega}_s = \hat{\omega}$<br>$R_{sb''} = R_{sb}R$:<br>rotate $\theta$ about $\hat{\omega}_b = \hat{\omega}$ | displacing a frame {b}:<br>$T = \begin{bmatrix} \mathrm{Rot}(\hat{\omega}, \theta) & p \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$<br>$T_{sb'} = TT_{sb}$: rotate $\theta$ about $\hat{\omega}_s = \hat{\omega}$ (moves {b} origin), translate $p$ in {s}<br>$T_{sb''} = T_{sb}T$: translate $p$ in {b}, rotate $\theta$ about $\hat{\omega}$ in new body frame |
| unit rotation axis is $\hat{\omega} \in \mathbb{R}^3$,<br>where $\|\hat{\omega}\| = 1$ | "unit" screw axis is $\mathcal{S} = \begin{bmatrix} \omega \\ v \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^6$,<br>where either (i) $\|\omega\| = 1$ or (ii) $\omega = 0$ and $\|v\| = 1$ |
|  | for a screw axis $\{q, \hat{s}, h\}$ with finite $h$,<br>$\mathcal{S} = \begin{bmatrix} \omega \\ v \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \hat{s} \\ -\hat{s} \times q + h\hat{s} \end{bmatrix}$ |
| angular velocity is $\omega = \hat{\omega}\dot{\theta}$ | twist is $\mathcal{V} = \mathcal{S}\dot{\theta}$ |
| for any 3-vector, e.g., $\omega \in \mathbb{R}^3$,<br>$[\omega] = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -\omega_3 & \omega_2 \\ \omega_3 & 0 & -\omega_1 \\ -\omega_2 & \omega_1 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \in so(3)$<br>identities, $\omega, x \in \mathbb{R}^3, R \in SO(3)$:<br>$[\omega] = -[\omega]^{\mathrm{T}}, [\omega]x = -[x]\omega,$<br>$[\omega][x] = ([x][\omega])^{\mathrm{T}}, R[\omega]R^{\mathrm{T}} = [R\omega]$ | for $\mathcal{V} = \begin{bmatrix} \omega \\ v \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^6$,<br>$[\mathcal{V}] = \begin{bmatrix} [\omega] & v \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix} \in se(3)$<br>(the pair $(\omega, v)$ can be a twist $\mathcal{V}$ or a "unit" screw axis $\mathcal{S}$, depending on the context) |
