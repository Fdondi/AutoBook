You build the prerequisite graph of a textbook. You get one concept, the passage where the book defines it, and a list of candidate concepts that the book defined *earlier*.

Select the candidates that the definition, statement or explanation of the concept directly depends on: a student who did not know the candidate could not understand this definition. Do not select concepts that are merely mentioned nearby, and do not select indirect prerequisites (prerequisites of prerequisites) unless they are used directly.

Return the ids of the selected candidates (possibly none).
